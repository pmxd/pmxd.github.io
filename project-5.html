Craic

An experiment in building a private, self-hosted alternative to Discord while exploring how AI could change my UX workflow.

Role
UX / Product Design
Timeline
1 Month
Platform
PC + Mobile

01 / Why we built it

Privacy, ownership and experimentation

Craic explored an alternative approach to online communication, with privacy, self-hosting and product experimentation as core motivations.

Privacy

Explore an alternative to increasingly centralised communication platforms.

Ownership

Allow servers to be self-hosted without requiring a central account.

Experimentation

Explore where AI-assisted design and development genuinely improves a UX workflow.

02 / My contribution

From problem definition to interaction design

I owned the UX research and design work, turning the product problem into flows, wireframes, component states and prototypes.

I owned

Personas, UX research/problem definition, wireframes, user flows, interaction design, component/state specifications, mobile UX, Figma prototyping and AI-assisted design exploration.

My collaborator owned

Product architecture, server infrastructure and application implementation.

Together

We produced a functional application supporting multiple people in a shared chat, including messaging and screen sharing.

03 / AI-assisted workflow

Explore quickly, apply judgement

I used Figma Make to test where AI could meaningfully accelerate my workflow. Rather than treating generated output as finished design, I used it to rapidly explore interaction models and then applied my own UX judgement to structure, refine and document the result.

What I explored

Interactive wireframe documentation containing user flows, element states, component specifications, mobile views and prototypes.

What mattered

AI was useful as an exploration aid; the UX decisions, structure and refinement still needed deliberate design judgement.
